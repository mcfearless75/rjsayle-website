Common Questions

Landlord Services FAQs

What is a CP12 certificate?

A CP12 (also called a Gas Safety Record) is a legal requirement for all rental properties with gas appliances. It must be carried out annually by a Gas Safe registered engineer. Tenants must receive a copy within 28 days of the inspection.

How quickly can you turn around a void property?

We can typically complete a boiler service and CP12 inspection within 48-72 hours of booking for properties in Wirral and Merseyside. Emergency same-day appointments may be available.

Do you offer discounts for landlords with multiple properties?

Yes — portfolio discounts are available for landlords with two or more properties. Contact us to discuss a tailored service contract.

Can you deal directly with tenants?

Yes. With landlord authorisation, we can liaise directly with tenants to arrange access, carry out works, and report back — reducing the management burden on you.

Are you on any approved contractor lists?

We are Gas Safe registered and fully insured. We are currently in the process of registering with CHAS to qualify for housing association and social landlord approved contractor frameworks.

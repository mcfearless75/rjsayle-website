The Boiler Upgrade Scheme in 2026
What Changed and What You Get

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme has moved on a lot this year — new grant tiers, no more EPC requirement, and a time-limited £9,000 rate for off-gas-grid homes. Here's what the scheme actually covers in plain English, and where R.J. Sayle can help.

Current Grant Amounts

What the Boiler Upgrade Scheme Pays

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS) is a government grant that reduces the upfront cost of a low-carbon heating installation. As of August 2026, four rates apply.

£7,500

Air Source / Ground Source Heat Pump

The standard rate for a fully installed air source or ground source heat pump, replacing a fossil fuel heating system.

£2,500

Air-to-Air Heat Pump

Newly eligible under SI 2026/390 — the first time air-to-air systems have qualified for BUS funding.

£5,000

Biomass Boiler

Available for eligible biomass boiler installations, typically on rural and off-grid properties.

£9,000

Off-Gas-Grid Rate

A time-limited enhanced rate for off-gas-grid properties. Runs 21 July 2026 to 31 March 2027 — see below.

Scheme Update

What Changed in 2026

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme has been reworked this year. Four changes matter most for homeowners in Wirral and Merseyside.

Air-to-air added at £2,500 (SI 2026/390)

Statutory Instrument 2026/390 brought air-to-air heat pumps into the scheme for the first time, at a £2,500 grant rate. Previously only air source, ground source and biomass systems qualified.

EPC no longer required to apply

A valid Energy Performance Certificate used to be a precondition of applying. That requirement has been dropped, removing a common stumbling block for older properties with lapsed or missing EPCs.

Capacity limit raised to 45kW

The maximum system capacity covered by the scheme has increased to 45kW for standard properties, 70kW for multi-unit properties, and 300kW for shared ground loop systems — opening the scheme to larger homes and small blocks.

Scheme extended to 2030

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme has been extended through to 2030, giving homeowners a longer runway to plan a low-carbon heating upgrade rather than rushing a decision.

Hard Deadline

The £9,000 Off-Grid Window — and Its Deadline

Properties that are off the mains gas grid can currently claim an enhanced £9,000 grant rate, rather than the standard £7,500. This is a genuine, time-limited window: it opened on 21 July 2026 and closes on 31 March 2027. After that date the rate reverts, so if this applies to your property, the clock is running.

"Off-gas-grid" simply means your property has no mains gas connection and currently relies on oil, LPG, electric storage heating, or a similar off-grid fuel source. Several rural pockets of Wirral — including areas toward Thurstaston, Barnston, Frankby and rural Neston — fall into this category, alongside off-grid properties elsewhere on the peninsula.

If you think your property might be off-grid and eligible, the sensible move is to check your current fuel type and start the conversation early. Grant funding pots can close before their advertised end date if demand is high, so leaving it until March 2027 to enquire is a risk.

Eligibility

Who Qualifies

Homeowners and landlords replacing a fossil fuel heating system (or, for the off-grid rate, an oil, LPG or electric system) with an eligible low-carbon system.

Properties in England and Wales with a domestic or, in some cases, small non-domestic setup within the scheme's capacity limits.

No valid EPC is required — this precondition was removed in the 2026 scheme rules.

Systems must fall within the capacity limits: up to 45kW for standard properties, 70kW for multi-unit properties, 300kW for shared ground loop systems.

The installation must be carried out by an MCS-certified installer and registered through the scheme process.

Off-grid properties applying between 21 July 2026 and 31 March 2027 can claim the enhanced £9,000 rate instead of the standard £7,500.

Process

How to Apply

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant is not something you claim yourself after the fact — it has to be applied for on your behalf by the installer carrying out the work, and that installer must hold current MCS certification. This is a fixed requirement of the scheme, not something we can advise around.

In practice, that means: get a survey and quote from an MCS-certified installer, agree the specification, and the installer applies for the grant voucher on your behalf before work begins. The grant amount is then deducted from your final invoice.

We can talk through whether your property might be a good candidate for the scheme and point you toward the right next steps, but we are not currently in a position to say we can carry out an MCS-eligible heat pump installation ourselves. Setting Expectations

What BUS Does NOT Cover — Gas Boilers

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme does not fund a standard like-for-like gas boiler replacement. It never has. The scheme exists specifically to support the switch to heat pumps and, in limited cases, biomass boilers — not to subsidise conventional gas heating.

This sometimes gets tangled up with a separate rumour: that gas boilers were about to be banned in existing homes. They are not, and were never going to be under current plans. The proposed 2035 phase-out for existing properties was dropped from the Warm Homes Plan, published 21 January 2026. It remains completely legal to install a replacement gas boiler in an existing home today, and no BUS grant applies either way.

For a lot of Wirral properties — particularly those on the mains gas grid with a heating system already suited to a boiler — a straightforward, well-specified gas boiler replacement is still the right, honest recommendation. If a heat pump grant genuinely suits your situation, we'll say so — and if a gas boiler is the better call, we'll say that instead.

Common Questions

Boiler Upgrade Scheme FAQs

Do I need a heat pump to get any of this money?

For most of the Boiler Upgrade Scheme, yes — the grant is aimed at air source heat pumps, ground source heat pumps and, since SI 2026/390, air-to-air heat pumps. Biomass boilers also qualify in specific circumstances, usually off the gas grid. There is no BUS grant for a straightforward like-for-like gas boiler replacement.

What does off-gas-grid mean?

It means your property has no mains gas connection and relies on oil, LPG, electric storage heaters or a similar off-grid fuel. Some rural pockets of the Wirral, including parts of Thurstaston, Barnston, Frankby and rural Neston, fall into this category. If you are unsure whether your property counts, check your current heating fuel and get in touch and we can help you work it out.

Does my installer need to be MCS certified?

Yes. To claim a Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant, the installation must be carried out and certified by an MCS-certified installer — this is a fixed requirement of the scheme, not optional. Always check current MCS certification status directly with the installer before booking any grant-funded work.

Can I still get a grant if my EPC has expired?

Yes. As of the 2026 scheme rules, a valid Energy Performance Certificate is no longer required to apply for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme. This removed one of the most common reasons applications used to stall.

Is the £9,000 off-grid rate guaranteed until the deadline, or could it end early?

The £9,000 rate is scheduled to run from 21 July 2026 to 31 March 2027, but government grant pots can close early if funding is exhausted before the end date. If you are off the gas grid and considering a heat pump, it is worth starting the conversation with an MCS-certified installer well before the deadline rather than close to it.

Is a gas boiler still legal to install in 2026?

Yes. The proposed 2035 phase-out of gas boilers in existing homes was dropped from the Warm Homes Plan, published 21 January 2026. It remains fully legal to fit a replacement gas boiler in an existing property today, and for many homes it is still the sensible, cost-effective choice.

Common Questions

Commercial Plumbing & Heating FAQs

What is a PPM contract?

A Planned Preventive Maintenance contract is a fixed-price annual agreement covering regular servicing, safety inspections, and priority callout for commercial premises. It removes the risk of unexpected repair costs and ensures your gas appliances remain compliant.

Do you carry out CP17 commercial gas safety inspections?

Yes — all commercial gas appliances must be inspected annually by a Gas Safe registered engineer. We issue CP17 certificates for offices, retail premises, care homes, and industrial units across Wirral and Merseyside.

Can you handle multi-site commercial projects?

Yes. We have experience coordinating heating and plumbing works across multiple locations and can manage larger programmes for landlords, developers, and property management companies.

Do you work with housing associations?

We work with social landlords, approved contractor frameworks, and housing association maintenance programmes. Contact us to discuss approved contractor registration.

What areas do you cover for commercial work?

Our primary commercial area is Wirral and Merseyside, but we accept larger commercial projects nationwide.

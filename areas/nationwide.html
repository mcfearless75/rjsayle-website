Nationwide Commercial Plumbing, Heating & Shopfitting

Based on the Wirral, we take on commercial fit-out, shopfitting and heating projects across the UK — retail units, hospitality venues, office premises and planned maintenance contracts. Gas Safe registered. Get in touch to discuss your project.

Our day-to-day base is the Wirral, and that's where the bulk of our domestic and commercial work happens. "Nationwide" here means something specific: for commercial projects — shopfitting, retail and hospitality fit-outs, office premises, planned maintenance contracts and large-scale heating system design — we're willing and equipped to travel further afield when the project justifies it. It isn't a claim to a network of branches or regional depots; it's one operation, based in one place, taking on work where the job is right.

On the commercial side, that covers plumbing and heating installation as part of a wider fit-out, first and second fix pipework, large-scale heating system design and installation for retail or hospitality premises, and ongoing planned maintenance contracts for businesses that want one contractor they can call rather than a rotating list of subcontractors. If a shop unit, restaurant, office or commercial premises needs plumbing and heating work done properly as part of a build-out or refit, that's the kind of project this covers.

The case for using a small, hands-on operation over a larger contractor is straightforward: the person quoting the job is the person who turns up and does the work. There's no handoff between an estimator, a project manager and a rotating crew — decisions on site get made by someone who understands the whole project, not just their slice of it. That matters more on fit-out and shopfitting work than almost anywhere else, where trades need to be coordinated tightly against a fixed opening date.

To discuss a commercial project outside the Wirral, call 07450 237593 or use the contact form with your location and a description of the work. We'll give you a straight answer on whether it's feasible before anything is quoted, and a fixed-price quote — including travel where relevant — once we understand the scope.

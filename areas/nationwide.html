Nationwide Plumbing & Heating

Our primary service area is the Wirral Peninsula and Merseyside — but R.J. Sayle Plumbing & Heating has the experience, qualifications and equipment to travel beyond this core region for the right project. Over two decades in the trade, we have undertaken work across the North West and beyond, and we are always happy to discuss projects that fall outside our usual geography.

Extended travel is most practical for larger or more specialist jobs where local alternatives may lack the expertise or equipment we can offer. This includes commercial gas safety inspections for businesses with multiple properties, specialist unvented cylinder installations, large commercial boiler replacements, or complex central heating system redesigns for substantial properties.

All our engineers hold full Gas Safe registration and carry the required qualifications for domestic, commercial and industrial gas work. We are experienced with a wide range of boiler makes and models, including older and unusual systems that some engineers will not touch. If you have a complex project that you are struggling to find the right contractor for, we would be delighted to hear from you.

To enquire about extended area coverage, please call us on 07450 237593 or complete our online contact form. Describe your project and location, and we will respond promptly with an honest assessment of whether we can help and, if so, a full fixed-price quotation.

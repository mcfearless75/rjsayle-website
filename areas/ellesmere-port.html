Ellesmere Port sits at the gateway between the Wirral Peninsula and Cheshire, and R.J. Sayle Plumbing & Heating has been serving homeowners and landlords across the town and its surrounding villages for over two decades. Whether you live in one of the residential estates off the A41, in the quieter village of Hooton or Childer Thornton, or in a newer development near the Cheshire Oaks retail park, our Gas Safe registered engineers are ready to help.

The town has a diverse mix of housing stock — from pre-war terraced streets near the historic dock area to post-war social housing estates and late twentieth-century detached homes on the outskirts. Many older properties still have older-style gravity-fed heating systems that lack the efficiency and reliability of modern combi or system boilers. Our engineers can assess your existing set-up and recommend a cost-effective upgrade that will reduce your bills and improve comfort.

We carry out boiler installations, boiler servicing, central heating repairs, landlord gas safety certificates (CP12), power flushing, bathroom and kitchen plumbing, and emergency call-outs across the whole Ellesmere Port area. All work is fully Gas Safe registered and comes with a written guarantee.

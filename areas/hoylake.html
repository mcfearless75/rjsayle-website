Plumbing & Heating in Hoylake

Hoylake is a charming seaside town on the north-west Wirral, famous internationally for its links golf course — the Royal Liverpool Golf Club — and beloved locally for its independent shops, cafes and excellent schools. The housing stock is varied: Victorian and Edwardian semis, period terraces, and a number of larger detached homes with considerable heating requirements.

R.J. Sayle Plumbing & Heating regularly works in Hoylake and neighbouring Meols, installing boilers, fitting radiators, carrying out power flushes and responding to emergency call-outs. Many older Hoylake properties have gravity-fed heating systems or older back boilers that are well past their best. We specialise in cost-effective, efficient upgrades that make a real difference to comfort and energy costs.

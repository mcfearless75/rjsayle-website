Liverpool is a city of tremendous character — from the Georgian townhouses of the Georgian Quarter and the Victorian terraces of Toxteth and Kensington, to the modern waterfront apartments and the leafy suburbs of Woolton, Allerton and Mossley Hill. R.J. Sayle Plumbing & Heating serves homeowners and landlords across the city and its surrounding suburbs, bringing Wirral-based expertise across the Mersey.

Liverpool's diverse housing stock presents a wide range of plumbing and heating challenges. Many older city-centre and inner-suburb properties still have outdated back boilers hidden behind gas fires, ageing gravity-fed systems with poor pressure, or original copper pipework approaching the end of its service life. Our Gas Safe registered engineers are experienced in working across all these property types, diagnosing problems accurately and recommending the most cost-effective solution.

We cover the whole of Liverpool for boiler installations, boiler repairs, annual servicing, landlord gas safety certificates, central heating system upgrades, power flushing and all general plumbing work including bathroom and kitchen installations. We also provide emergency call-outs across Liverpool for urgent situations such as burst pipes, boiler breakdowns and suspected gas leaks.

All work is carried out by fully qualified, Gas Safe registered engineers and is backed by written guarantees. We pride ourselves on transparent fixed pricing — the price we quote is the price you pay.

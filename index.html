We install all leading boiler brands including Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Baxi, Ideal and Glow-worm. We will advise on the best boiler for your property, usage and budget. Manufacturer warranties of up to 12 years are available on certain Worcester Bosch models when installed by an approved installer.

What is a power flush and do I need one?

A power flush is a deep clean of your central heating system that uses high-velocity water and specialist chemicals to remove sludge, rust and debris. Signs you may need one include: radiators cold at the bottom, boiler making banging or kettling noises, slow heating, or cloudy/discoloured water from bleed valves. A power flush significantly improves efficiency and is often required before a new boiler installation.

The Basics

Combi, System or Conventional — the Short Version

The tool above weighs these against your bathrooms, pressure and property size. For the fuller explanation, including the process and what a survey covers, see our boiler installation page.

Combi

One unit, no tanks or cylinder, heats water on demand from the mains. Straightforward to install and the most common fit for one-bathroom homes.

System + cylinder

Stores hot water under mains pressure in a cylinder, so two or more bathrooms can run together without one going cold. See our unvented cylinder page.

Conventional

The traditional loft tank plus cylinder setup — sometimes the right call where mains pressure genuinely rules out the other two, or on a like-for-like replacement.

Common Questions

Boiler Selector FAQs

Is the result an exact quote?

No — it's a boiler type recommendation based on four quick answers, not a priced quote. We don't publish instant prices because the real cost depends on your existing pipework, controls and the property itself, which we can only assess properly on a survey. The tool gets you talking to us about the right thing, not a number pulled out of thin air.

How accurate is the recommendation?

It's built on the same reasoning we use on site — bathroom count, mains pressure and property size are the biggest factors in choosing a boiler type. It's a genuinely useful starting point for most homes, but it can't measure your actual flow rate or inspect your pipework, so some answers come back as "worth a quick chat" rather than a firm pick. That's deliberate — we'd rather say that honestly than guess.

What happens after I get my result?

Call 07450 237593 or use the "Get a Free Fixed Quote" button to send your details through the contact page. Either way you'll speak to Russ Sayle directly — not a call centre — to arrange a survey. The survey confirms (or corrects) the tool's suggestion and is what the fixed price is based on.

Do you install whichever boiler type the tool recommends?

We install combi, system and conventional boilers, plus unvented cylinders where a system boiler needs one. If the survey turns up something the tool couldn't know about — genuinely weak mains flow, an odd pipe run, a conservation-area constraint — we'll say so and explain why a different option suits your home better.
